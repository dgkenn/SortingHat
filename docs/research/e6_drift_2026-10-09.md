# E6 temporal miscalibration: diagnosis (2026-10-09)

Numbers-free summary. The aggregate tables (small-cell suppressed) are kept out of git under `out/diag/` (CLAUDE.md rule 6);
regenerate with `scripts/heedb_run.sh python3 scripts/diag_e6_drift.py --s3 --also-n-bins 10`.

**Symptom.** In the exploratory silver feasibility report, E6 (infectious/septic) had observed/expected far above 1 in the
temporal holdout (train early, test late within site) for both baseline-only and EEG models, while leave-one-site-out O/E was
close to 1.

**Finding.** Not a prevalence drift: E6 can only be ascertained in the late part of each site's record period, because the
blood-culture leg of the anchor has (near-)no in-window culture rows early on and then plateaus. The antibiotic-days leg rises
with it; organ dysfunction, lactate, antimicrobial and vasopressor availability, age, severity, sedation and visit timing are
flat. E1/E2/E5 move little between train and test. Where culture rows exist early, their timestamps sit days after t0 and only
move toward t0 late, consistent with result-time rather than collection-time stamps (inferred from aggregates).

| Hypothesis | Verdict |
|---|---|
| (a) ascertainment change (culture data availability) | supported |
| (b) date-shift artefact | not supported (coverage complete, drug/lab timing relative to t0 constant) |
| (c) coding / timestamp-meaning change | partial (culture timestamp semantics; late feed change) |
| (d) real case-mix change | not supported as explanation |

**Missing culture data is currently coded as a confident negative, not as not-assessable.**

**Recommendations** (not yet implemented):
1. Mark E6 not-assessable where the culture source cannot fire (per-site rule on the within-site t0-rank region with
   negligible in-window culture rows).
2. Until then, report E6 under leave-one-site-out only; treat temporal E6 O/E, Delta and AUROC as feed-era artefacts.
3. Human check (outside agent sessions) whether early culture rows carry result time rather than collection time; if so, take
   culture timing from collection/order time.
4. Add a label drift screen to the feasibility report (flag test/train prevalence ratio > 3 or < 1/3).
