## Edits needed in the plan

1. Line 62 (open-citation warning): CDS January 2026 guidance is now verified; replace with a citation (docket FDA-2017-D-6569, January 2026, reissued 29 Jan 2026). Keep the human-factors, CLEF and DUA items flagged, restated as below.
2. Human-factors guidance: rewrite as "Applying Human Factors and Usability Engineering to Medical Devices (final update, 3 Aug 2026)" plus the May 2026 submission-content guidance. Remove the implication that it is AI-specific. Point AI human-factors expectations to the Jan 2025 draft AI-enabled device guidance. Replace secondary citations with the FDA page.
3. CLEF: describe as "MIT CSAIL / Brigham / MGH authors (Cao ... Katabi), pretrained on HEEDB", not as a BDSP-group model. Weights remain unconfirmed; keep it off the manifest (l.439 is correct). Read Appendix H, or email the authors, before relying on it.
4. BDSP restricted DUA claim: delete "one DUA covers every restricted dataset". Replace with "each restricted dataset (I-CARE, SPaRCNet) requires its own signed DUA under the BDSP Restricted Health Data License 1.0.0, which limits use to non-commercial research". Add this licence limit to the commercial-track discussion, since restricted and credentialed data cannot back a commercial build.
5. Line 51 (Ceribell): keep, but add "510(k), 10 Aug 2026, portal software enhancements" and attribute the 1 million hours to the company's description of the training data. Cite the 9 Dec 2025 delirium clearance. Obtain 510(k) numbers from accessdata.fda.gov before the Pre-Sub.
6. Lines 54 and 333 (882.1450): soften "limited to structural injury in trauma" to "covers an interpretation of the structural condition of the brain; the only cleared indications are adult closed head injury (BrainScope)". Quote the eCFR text verbatim. Add product code PIW.
7. Line 55 (BrainScope): add the dates (DEN140025, granted 17 Nov 2014) and note later PIW 510(k)s (K161068, K181785). Do not call the Ahead 100 a 510(k).
8. Manifest, row by row: HEEDB v4.1 and BIND v1.0 are correct (state "Credentialed"). MORGOTH 1.0.0 and NESI 1.0.0 are correct (NESI full name: Neurophysiologic Encephalopathy Severity Index); remove "download weights" for MORGOTH unless the release lists them. I-CARE: confirm 7 hospitals and note v2.0 was published 29 Jul 2026. SPaRCNet: "1.1" is the DUA/licence version, not the dataset. TME cohort: rename to the actual project title ("Epileptiform activity and outcomes in toxic-metabolic encephalopathy", Software, n=121) and confirm it contains EEG. GCS-from-EHR: confirm the BDSP project page and title. TDBRAIN: change source to Brainclinics, "DUA V3", and drop "V3.1" unless a source is found. VitalDB: remove "conflicting terms" or cite the specific conflict (PhysioNet shows CC BY 4.0). Change the Access column from "Verify in BDSP" to the actual tier (credentialed vs restricted).
9. CBraMod rows: cite TUEG-only pretraining to the CBraMod paper itself, not secondary work; verify TUSZ/TUAB/TUEV overlap and the Hugging Face model-card licence (repo is MIT) before stating "contaminated" or "commercial candidate".
10. Regulatory framing (l.333): add that QMSR has been in effect since 2 Feb 2026 (so "from the first commercial line of code" is correct as written), and cite the Dec 2024 final PCCP guidance by its full title.
