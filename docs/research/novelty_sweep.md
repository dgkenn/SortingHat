# Sorting Hat - Phase 0c Novelty Sweep

Date of sweep: 2026-10-07. Tools: PubMed (E-utilities wrapper), WebSearch/WebFetch (arXiv, medRxiv, AES/AAN listings, FDA 510(k) summaries, Google Patents). About 30 searches. Every row below carries an identifier that was actually returned by a tool in this session. Anything I could not locate is labelled UNVERIFIED rather than filled in.

## (a) Bottom line

1. The core question (calibrated, multi-label, cross-site EEG differential of the CAUSE of acute impaired consciousness, beyond severity and sedation) is not answered or claimed by anything I found, 2022-2026.
2. Closest prior art is small, single-site or single-trial, and single-label: a Korean 3-way CNN (NCSE / metabolic / benzodiazepine, AUC 0.905), the CERTA 4-class CNN (accuracy 54.5%), and two Brazilian coma-etiology CNN-LSTMs (N=50).
3. None is calibrated, none is multi-label, none controls for severity or sedative exposure, none is site-held-out. Several are circular by design (labels or classes partly defined by EEG).
4. Scoop risk: LOW-MODERATE for the full design as written; MODERATE for a weaker version. The realistic threat is the BDSP/HEEDB ecosystem. CLEF (arXiv 2605.10817, May 2026) already pretrains on HEEDB and benchmarks 234 ICD-phecode tasks including "anoxic brain injury", "cerebral edema", "cirrhosis" and sedative medications. A phecode-derived "etiology" result could appear at any time, with weaker labels.
5. Ceribell has FDA-cleared AI for delirium (K251936, Dec 2025) and seizure/epileptiform detection (Aug 2026). I found no etiology or encephalopathy-subtype claim from Ceribell or any other rapid-EEG vendor. Moat is label quality, circularity control, and calibration, not the idea.

## (c) The Korean cohort and CERTA

### Korean cohort (identified, confirmed)
- Citation: Kim YT, Kim H, So M, Kong J, Kim KT, Hong JH, Son Y, Sa JK, Do S, Han JH, Kim JB. "Differentiating loss of consciousness causes through artificial intelligence-enabled decoding of functional connectivity." NeuroImage 2024;297:120749. Published 2024-07-19.
- IDs: PMID 39033787; DOI 10.1016/j.neuroimage.2024.120749; PII S1053-8119(24)00246-5.
- Matches the plan's description: NCSE vs metabolic encephalopathy vs benzodiazepine intoxication; CNN on 3D FC adjacency matrices achieved AUC 0.905; 20 s epochs optimal; accuracy "maintained in a prospective cohort"; delta and theta bands most discriminative. Cohort N and per-class AUROCs are not in the abstract (full text not read).
- Corresponding author as listed: Jung Bin Kim, MD, Department of Neurology, Korea University Anam Hospital, Korea University College of Medicine, 73 Goryeodae-ro, Seongbuk-gu, Seoul 02841, Republic of Korea. Public email as printed in the PubMed record ("Electronic address"): kjbin80@korea.ac.kr.

- Note for Study 2: the plan treats this as a "replication of E3 vs E5 vs E4a" stress test. ### CERTA (identified; the "14 etiologies" detail was NOT confirmed)
- CERTA = Continuous EEG Randomized Trial in Adults, NCT03129438. Pragmatic multicenter RCT, 4 tertiary hospitals in Switzerland (Lausanne/CHUV, Bern/Inselspital, Basel, Sion), 2017-2018, 364 analyzed adults, GCS <=11 or FOUR <=12, no recent seizure/SE, cEEG 30-48 h vs two 20-min routine EEGs, primary outcome 6-month mortality.

## (e) Gaps this project uniquely fills

1. A genuinely multi-label, calibrated differential (proper scoring rules, log loss, per-label calibration) instead of single-label argmax accuracy or AUC. Nothing found reports calibration for etiology.
2. Incremental value over what the team already knows. No prior work uses severity (GCS/FOUR/NESI), t0 sedative exposure, bedside/lab/imaging baselines, or a referral-indication baseline. All prior etiology classifiers can be winning on severity or drug state.
3. Circularity control. The Korean and CERTA classes (NCSE) are partly EEG-defined; ICD/phecode labels (CLEF) are partly derived from EEG-reading clinicians. The banned-evidence rule, objective-anchor requirement, and silver-vs-gold circularity audit are new.
4. Split of intoxication (E4a) vs iatrogenic sedation (E4b). Prior work lumps "benzodiazepine intoxication" or sedatives as a class or as a medication task.
5. Site-held-out evaluation with a favorable effect required at every site. Prior work is single-center or single-trial; Jonas 2022 and Kim 2024 are internal.
6. Breadth of etiologies. Prior work covers 3-4 classes; sepsis-associated encephalopathy, CNS infection, hypoxic-ischemic, structural, and metabolic have never been jointly modeled with adjudicated gold labels.
7. Time-to-signal and deployable-montage analysis (2 min vs 10 min; real rapid-headband geometry). Kim 2024 shows 20 s epochs work in a 3-class problem, which supports H6 but does not test rapid-headset montages.
8. Commercial-clean gap (frozen CBraMod vs MORGOTH-style encoders on the same task). Zare 2026 (arXiv 2607.24519) gives independent support for running this comparison and for dataset-identity leakage probes.
9. Regulatory framing around Ceribell: Ceribell's cleared claims are delirium and epileptiform detection; etiologic classification remains unclaimed.
