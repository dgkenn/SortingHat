
Sorting Hat — Research Plan
EEG-based probabilistic etiologic differential for acute impaired consciousness
 · 
Executive summary
The project is worth pursuing, and the master handoff (v1.0, 29 Sep 2026) is already a strong retrospective protocol. This plan keeps its spine and fixes the problems most likely to sink it: label circularity, sedation inflating the result, unverified timestamps, an unbudgeted adjudication workload, and a commercial path that ignores the rapid-EEG hardware it depends on.
The question. Does the first 10 minutes of scalp EEG add calibrated, cross-site information about why an adult is acutely unconscious, beyond how deeply unconscious they are and what the team already knows at that moment?
The sequence. Phase 0 feasibility gates → Study 1 HEEDB discovery (site-held-out, research-only) → Study 2 external stress tests and deployable-montage simulation → commercial track (clean encoder plus a licensed proprietary etiologic cohort) → prospective silent validation → human-in-the-loop study → utility RCT → FDA. Nothing commercial gets built until Study 1 passes Gate 2.
The five changes that matter most:
Split toxic/pharmacologic into exogenous intoxication (an etiology) and iatrogenic sedation (a known state modifier), and put t0 sedative exposure into the Study 1A baseline. Otherwise EEG "wins" by detecting propofol the team already gave.
Ban EEG-contaminated evidence from silver labels: EEG reports, note sentences citing EEG, and nonspecific encephalopathy codes. Otherwise the model learns to imitate the EEG reader's guess, not the cause.
Make Phase 0 a field-level audit of HEEDB timestamps (result availability, medication administration, imaging finalization, ACI onset). Study 1B is impossible without them.
Budget adjudication honestly. As written it is roughly 4,500 chart reviews and 1,100–1,900 physician-hours; dual review moves to the evaluation set only, and packets are pre-assembled locally.
Treat rapid EEG as the deployment reality. Simulate real rapid-headset electrode geometries rather than generic 12/8/6/4-channel subsets, and run prospective work where rapid EEG is already standard care.
Critical evaluation of the source material
The science is well framed: multi-label etiology, a frozen t0, site-held-out evaluation, proper scoring rules, and STARD-AI/TRIPOD+AI reporting are all correct choices. The weaknesses are in what the model can cheat on, what the labels secretly contain, and what the plan assumes about data, labor and the market.

Core question, claims and hypotheses
Two claims are tested, in order, and the second is the one a product depends on.
Scientific claim (Study 1A). Etiologic families of acute impaired consciousness leave reproducible, site-transportable EEG signatures beyond severity and known sedative exposure.
Translational claim (Study 1B). Adding a short EEG to everything the team knows at t0 improves the calibrated, multi-label etiologic differential.
Primary endpoint (both studies). Change in mean masked multi-label log loss on site-held-out predictions, negative meaning EEG helps:
Here L_i is patient i's set of assessable labels and ℓ is binary log loss. Change from the handoff: the primary label set excludes E3 (epileptic contributor) and E4b (iatrogenic sedation). E3 is partly defined by EEG, so including it guarantees a win; it becomes the pipeline's positive control instead. E4b is known to the team at t0 and is not a diagnostic question.

Program architecture
The program runs as two legally separate lineages, and the only thing allowed across is what has been published. That makes the firewall auditable: anyone can check that a commercial design decision traces to a public document.
The research track answers whether the signal exists; the commercial track builds the product from green data once it does.

Study 1: HEEDB retrospective discovery
Study 1 keeps the handoff's cohort, window, preprocessing and representation ladder. It changes the label set, the silver-label rules, the adjudication design and the baselines.
Population and index time
Adults, acute-care encounter, first qualifying EEG per patient; strict cohort GCS ≤11 or FOUR ≤12, broad EHR-phenotype cohort reported separately.
t0 = EEG start. Primary window is minutes 1–11; at least 60% usable data on the minimum channel set.
EEG within 24 h of documented ACI onset, with time since onset as a covariate and ≤6/12/48 h sensitivity analyses.
New: record the EEG referral indication as a category (e.g. "rule out NCSE", "post-arrest", "unexplained AMS"). It defines spectrum and powers Baseline D.

Reference standard
Gold labels use the handoff's ordinal states (absent / possible / probable / definite / unassessable), blinded to EEG, reports and model outputs.
Change: dual independent review plus third-reader resolution on the evaluation set only. Development cases get one reviewer, with a random 20% double-read to track agreement.
Packets are assembled by deterministic extraction plus an open-weight summarizer on approved compute. A sentence filter strips anything mentioning EEG before the reviewer sees it. Validate the summarizer against full charts on 50 cases before relying on it.
Silver labels: the circularity rules
Silver labels train models; they never score them. Three kinds of evidence are banned as positive evidence for E1, E2, E4a, E5, E6 and E7:
EEG reports, and any note sentence mentioning EEG, cEEG, LTM, slowing, triphasic waves, burst suppression, LPDs/GPDs/LRDA.
Nonspecific encephalopathy diagnosis codes (the G92 and G93.4 families) and free-text "toxic-metabolic encephalopathy" without an objective anchor.
Post-t0 neurology impressions, unless the sentence filter has removed EEG content.
Each positive needs an objective anchor: a lab value with timing, an imaging finding, a culture or CSF result, an arrest event, a toxicology result, or an antidote response. Circularity audit: if a silver-trained model agrees more with EEG-report impressions than with gold labels, the silver labels are leaking and get rebuilt.

Representations and models
The handoff's ladder stands: prior → qEEG → connectivity → MORGOTH findings → frozen embeddings → dynamics → combined, all with the same shallow multi-label head. Change: run frozen CBraMod embeddings on HEEDB alongside MORGOTH. The difference in Δ between them is the "commercial-clean gap", the single most useful number for the commercial decision.

Leave-one-site-out across adult sites; every held-out site must show a favorable Δ. Add a late-calendar temporal holdout within sites.
Mandatory controls from the handoff: severity matching, sedative-excluded subset, site/duration/channel leakage probes, MORGOTH exposure accounting, negative controls.

Optional Study 1D: retrospective reader study
Run only if H2 succeeds. Three clinicians (ED, ICU, neurology) give a probabilistic differential on 150 evaluation packets, then revise after seeing the model output. This yields the first human-plus-AI evidence and a direct clinician comparator two years before any prospective study.

Why rapid-EEG sites. Consecutive enrollment with EEG inside 30–60 minutes is not achievable with conventional EEG at most hospitals. Where rapid EEG is already ordered for AMS, the research adds only data capture and a hidden model. That makes a waiver or alteration of consent far more plausible than research-only EEG on incapacitated patients, which needs surrogate consent and biases who gets enrolled.
Regulatory framing. The nearest classification, 21 CFR 882.1450, covers only structural injury in trauma, so a multi-etiology aid is likely a new De Novo classification. Frame the first claim narrowly as an adjunctive aid that estimates probabilities of predefined etiologic families, never as a diagnosis. Use a Predetermined Change Control Plan if the model will update after authorization, and run design controls under the QMSR from the first commercial line of code.
De-risked means P2 shows four things at once: it works in consecutive patients, at hospitals never used in training, beyond what clinicians already know, with probabilities calibrated well enough to say both "likely" and "I don't know".
